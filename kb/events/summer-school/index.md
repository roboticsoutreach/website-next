# Summer School
The Computing Engineering and Robotics (CER) Summer School is an annual Summer School that we now hold in collaboration with the University of Southampton School of Electronics and Computing (ECS). This is usually held at the beginning of August as a non-residential event for secondary school leavers and sixth formers.

During this weeklong event, students are placed into to teams and are given a week to design, create and program a robot. These robots would then compete against other teams in a game that was designed by us. In addition to the main events, we deliver a range of workshops covering the different aspects of robot development, including software, mechanical design, and electronics.

Furthermore, along with the ECS department: academic talks, tours of various sceintific facilities and workshops on applying for university engineering courses are provided to inspire sutdents to pursue STEM.

The aim is to have teams leave the competition having had fun, but also having developed skills that will help them pursue degrees and careers in STEM fields.

## Responsibilities
SRO has a number of responsibilities during the event, including:

- Management of participant registration for the summer school
- Ensuring the safeguarding of students both in labs and during the event
- Planning for the week, including preparation the weekend before, the course itself, and tear down the following weekend
- Creating a game that is both challenging and achievable, and which offers a fair but competitive environment
- Providing technical advice to teams where neccessary
- Providing teams with a workspace, tools, and kit to build their robot
- Producing material (usually in the form of lab sessions) which will teach teams the skills that they need to be successful in the competition.

:::info
Prior to 2025, the summer school event was called the "Smallpeice Summer School" after our main partner - [Smallpeice Trust](https://www.smallpeicetrust.org.uk/) - which played a critical part in organising and running the event. Whilst our partnership has since ended, you might find references of the charity in past material.
:::
