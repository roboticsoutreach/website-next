# Places

The society makes use of many spaces around the university.

## Visitors

Access for visitors is by prior arrangement only. No public right of access exists within university buildings.

If you are helping us with an event and are not a member of the university you must either be accompanied within university buildings or have signed out an external ID card.

## Hours

Standard building hours are 08:00-18:00 Monday-Friday. Access outside these times is by ID card only.

Access overnight between the hours of 23:00 and 06:00 (or 08:00 for students) requires a special Out-of-Hours Working Permit which must be signed by your Head of Department/School and provided to University Security.

Note that student ID cards do not work until 08:00 and do not work during closure days and public holidays. If you require access early in the morning or during a closure period then you must either arrange this in advance or find a member of staff to escort you.
